# Module 1: Capital Gains Tax Analysis

## Objective

Compare two hypothetical property sale sequences to evaluate their potential effects on capital gains tax and combined after-tax proceeds.

## Strategies

- **Strategy A:** Sell Hillstate first, then Jungang Heights.
- **Strategy B:** Sell Jungang Heights first, then Hillstate.

## Information Required

For each property:
- Acquisition date
- Acquisition price
- Hypothetical sale price
- Ownership and residence history
- Property location
- Potentially deductible expenses

Additional information about household property ownership and applicable tax rules will be documented as needed.

## Analysis Process

1. Gather and organize property information.
2. Identify the tax rules applicable to each hypothetical sale date.
3. Estimate capital gains tax for each transaction.
4. Compare the two sale sequences.
5. Document assumptions, sources, and limitations.
