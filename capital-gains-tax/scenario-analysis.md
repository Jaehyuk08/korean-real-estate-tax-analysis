# Korean Real Estate Capital Gains Tax Scenario Analysis

## 1. Project Objective

This project compares two hypothetical residential property sale sequences in South Korea to investigate how sale order and timing may affect capital gains tax and combined after-tax proceeds.

The analysis focuses on two properties: Hillstate and Jungang Heights.

The tax figures in this document are preliminary calculator estimates and have not yet been independently verified.

## 6. Comparison Status

The two sale strategies have not yet been compared using fully verified and consistent tax assumptions.

A valid comparison must account for:
- The household's property ownership status at each sale.
- Applicable tax rules on each transaction date.
- Eligibility for exemptions and the long-term holding special deduction.
- Any applicable multi-home surcharge or temporary relief.
- Deductible acquisition, improvement, and sale expenses.
- Local income tax and other relevant tax components.

No final conclusion about the financially preferable sale sequence has been reached.
